## How to Use

Each Lab includes:
- **Original publication link**: Read it once to understand the author's design decisions
- **Your task**: Reproduce the result yourself without looking at the answer
- **Acceptance criteria**: Concrete visual + performance targets
- **Key questions**: Forces you to think about *why*, not just *how*
